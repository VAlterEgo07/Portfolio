Proyecto Mythologiae

A systems-driven open-world RPG blending classical mythology with a melancholic, Souls-like narrative. I serve as the Narrative Director and General Programmer.

The Premise: The Great Disappearance

Multiple ancient civilizations—Greeks, Romans, Celts, and Vikings—vanished abruptly from Earth. They were transported to an isolated dimension known as the "Nexus" after the chaotic activation of an ancient artifact: the Sphere of Chaos.

World-Building & Lore

The universe is governed by primordial forces: Order (Zadkiel), Chaos (Lotan), and Life (Behemoth). The player takes on the role of Kylon Drakon, a young investigator who accidentally activates the Sphere and wakes up 300 years later in this fractured, myth-filled world.

The Regions of the Nexus

The Game Design Document (GDD) details a vast world divided into culturally distinct regions, each with its own economy, politics, and boss encounters:

  • Romula: A city of gladiators where the Senate fights the Pro-Greek rebels.
  • Ordo Draconum: A medieval volcanic fortress guarded by the dragon Sarganthor.
  • Coileáni: A mystical Celtic swamp city ruled by druids and spiritual trials.
  • Delta Frost: A frozen Viking tundra fraught with ice golems and the illusions of Loki.
  • More...

Game Design Systems

Beyond the narrative, the GDD outlines complex gameplay mechanics including a 6-pillar elemental reaction system (Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Light, Darkness), dynamic combat mounts, and regional economies with fluctuating currency values.
