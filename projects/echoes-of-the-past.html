Echoes of the Past

A unique prototype blending 3D exploration with retro 2D combat, deeply rooted in Spanish folklore and mythology.

Spanish Folklore & Hybrid Aesthetics

The game features a striking visual contrast: players navigate through immersive 3D environments, but the characters, enemies, and UI elements transition into a nostalgic, retro 2D pixel-art style. The entire world, including the narrative and boss designs, draws heavy inspiration from the rich and dark tales of Spanish mythology.

Cassette-Based Combat System

The core combat logic revolves around a unique mechanical twist: using retro MP3 cassette tapes to trigger actions, attacks, and abilities. This modular system required precise programming to handle state management, combat flow, and the hybrid 2D/3D visual feedback.
